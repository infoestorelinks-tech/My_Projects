FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Which IT course is best after 10th class?+
After 10th, you can join Polytechnic Diploma in Computer Science (3 years) or Intermediate (MPC/BiPC with Computer Science). You can also start learning basic programming (Python, HTML) on free platforms like freeCodeCamp and Codecademy alongside your studies.
What degree is needed to become a Software Developer?+
A B.Tech/BE in Computer Science or IT is the most common path. However, BCA, B.Sc Computer Science, or even non-CS degrees work if you build strong coding skills. Many top developers are self-taught through online courses and personal projects.
How much does a fresher Software Engineer earn in India?+
Freshers typically earn ₹3–6 LPA at mid-level IT companies. At top product companies like Google, Microsoft, or Flipkart, fresher packages can range from ₹15–45 LPA. Skills, projects, and college reputation influence the package significantly.
Is coding required for all IT careers?+
Not at all! Many IT careers like UI/UX Design, Product Management, Cyber Security (non-coding roles), IT Project Management, Technical Writing, and IT Sales require minimal or no coding. However, basic coding knowledge is beneficial in any IT role.
What is the scope of AI careers in India?+
AI is the fastest-growing IT domain in India. India is expected to have over 1 million AI-related jobs by 2027. AI Engineers, ML Engineers, and Data Scientists command salaries of ₹8–40 LPA even at the junior-mid level. The demand far exceeds supply currently.
Which programming language should a beginner learn first?+
Python is universally recommended for beginners due to its simple, English-like syntax. It's used in AI/ML, web development, data science, and automation. After Python basics, you can specialize: JavaScript for web, Java/Kotlin for apps, SQL for databases.

🗺️ Your IT Career Roadmap

Follow a clear, step-by-step path from your current education level to a rewarding IT career. Designed for Indian students.

🎓 After 10th Class — IT Roadmap

Two main paths: Polytechnic Diploma or Intermediate. Both lead to strong IT careers.

📚
Step 1 — Choose Your Path
After 10th: Two Main Options
Option A: Intermediate (11th–12th) with MPC + Computer Science
Option B: Polytechnic Diploma in Computer Science (3 years)
Both paths lead to excellent IT careers — choose based on interest
If you love maths and theory, go Intermediate → B.Tech/BCA
If you want faster entry into work, Polytechnic gives lateral entry to B.Tech 2nd year
💻
Step 2 — Start Learning Now (Free)
Basic Computer Skills to Build Alongside Studies
Learn touch typing — aim for 40+ words per minute
MS Office basics — Word, Excel, PowerPoint
Introduction to the Internet, email, and cloud tools (Google Drive)
Start Python basics on freeCodeCamp or Scratch for programming concepts
freeCodeCamp Khan Academy Scratch.mit.edu YouTube: CS50
🎯
Step 3 — Intermediate / Diploma (2–3 years)
Build Your Foundation in Technology
Score well in core subjects (Maths, Science, Computer Science)
Learn HTML/CSS for web basics — build your first website
Practice Python/C++ programming — solve 100+ problems on HackerRank
Learn MS Excel advanced features — pivot tables, VLOOKUP
Take free certifications from Google, Microsoft, or NASSCOM
Coursera (free audit) W3Schools HackerRank Google Career Certificates
🏆
Step 4 — Degree / Entry IT Job
Choose Your IT Specialization
Join B.Tech CSE / IT / BCA / B.Sc CS for deeper knowledge
Start your IT career after Polytechnic Diploma (junior developer roles)
Build a portfolio of 3–5 real projects to show employers
Apply for internships in your 1st or 2nd year of degree

🎓 After Intermediate — IT Roadmap

Choose the right degree and start building real IT skills that employers value.

🎓
Step 1 — Choose Your IT Degree
Best Degree Options for IT Careers
B.Tech/BE Computer Science & Engineering — Best for Software/AI roles
B.Tech Information Technology — Focus on systems and networks
BCA (Bachelor of Computer Applications) — 3-year practical focused
B.Sc Computer Science — Strong theoretical base, good for research
B.Sc Data Science — Specialized for Data/AI careers (newer, high demand)
💡
Step 2 — 1st & 2nd Year (Build Foundations)
Master Core Programming and Tools
Master at least one language: Python, Java, or C++ (complete level)
Learn Data Structures and Algorithms — key for all top company interviews
Complete a full web development course (HTML, CSS, JavaScript basics)
Get comfortable with Linux/Terminal commands
Start using Git and GitHub for version control
NPTEL LeetCode The Odin Project Udemy
🔧
Step 3 — 3rd Year (Specialize and Build Portfolio)
Pick Your Domain and Build Real Projects
Choose specialization: Web Dev / Data Science / Cyber Security / Cloud / Mobile
Build 3 real projects and host them on GitHub
Get your first certification (AWS Cloud Practitioner, Google IT Support, etc.)
Apply for internships on Internshala, LinkedIn, or company websites
Participate in hackathons (Smart India Hackathon, Flipkart Grid, etc.)
AWS Free Tier Kaggle GitHub Internshala
🚀
Step 4 — Final Year (Job-Ready)
Prepare for Placements and Campus Interviews
Complete your final year project — make it industry-relevant (AI/Cloud/Mobile)
Prepare for campus recruitment: DSA, OS, DBMS, Networking, Aptitude
Register on LinkedIn, create a professional profile with your projects
Apply off-campus at TCS, Infosys, Wipro, Amazon, Flipkart, startups
Target ₹3–6 LPA service companies and ₹8–25 LPA product companies

🎓 After Degree — IT Roadmap

You have a degree. Now sharpen your edge with specialized skills and strategic career moves.

🔍
Step 1 — Assessment
Identify Your Skill Gaps and Target Role
Take our Career Quiz to identify your strongest IT domain
Compare your current skills with job descriptions on LinkedIn and Naukri
Identify your target role and top companies you want to work at
Set a realistic 6-month upskilling plan
📜
Step 2 — Upskill with Certifications (3–6 months)
Industry-Recognized Certifications
Cloud: AWS Solutions Architect / GCP Associate / Azure Fundamentals
Data/AI: IBM Data Science, Google Data Analytics, TensorFlow Developer
Cyber Security: CompTIA Security+, CEH (Certified Ethical Hacker), CISSP
Web: Meta Front-End Developer, Full Stack on Coursera or Udemy
Management: PMP, Agile/Scrum Master (for PM/Product roles)
Coursera edX Udemy LinkedIn Learning
🏗️
Step 3 — Build Portfolio
Create Real Projects That Impress Recruiters
Build 5 projects showcasing your target domain skills
Contribute to open-source projects on GitHub
Write technical blogs on Medium or Hashnode to showcase expertise
Deploy all projects live (Vercel, Netlify, AWS free tier)
💼
Step 4 — Higher Studies Option
M.Tech / MCA / MBA (Tech) for Advanced Roles
M.Tech CSE (GATE): Best for R&D, IIT, NIT level roles, ₹15–40 LPA starting
MCA (Master of Computer Applications): Strong coding + applications focus
M.Sc Data Science / AI: For specialized high-pay AI/ML roles
MBA (Tech/Business): For Product Manager, IT Manager, and leadership roles
Online Masters: Georgia Tech OMSCS ($8K total), IIT Madras BS Data Science
🌍
Step 5 — Career Acceleration
Grow Fast in Your IT Career
Network actively on LinkedIn — connect with engineers at target companies
Attend tech meetups, DevFests, AWS Community Days in your city
Apply for remote global jobs after 2–3 years experience (3–5× salary)
Consider freelancing for extra income while building experience
Mentor juniors and speak at college tech fests to build your brand
